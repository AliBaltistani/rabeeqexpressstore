# How to Get Your Google Maps API Key 🗺️

This checklist is written completely for non-technical beginners. Follow it step-by-step, and you'll have your API key ready to send to your developer in less than 5 minutes!

### Step 1: Sign in to Google Cloud
1. Open your web browser and go to [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/).
2. Sign in using your normal Gmail account.

### Step 2: Create a Project
1. Look at the top-left corner (next to the Google Cloud logo) and click on the **Select a project** dropdown.
2. Click the **New Project** button in the top right of the popup window.
3. Give it a simple name like "My Store Maps" and click **Create**.

### Step 3: Turn on the Map Services
Your project needs to tell Google that you intend to use Maps.
1. In the big search bar at the very top of the page, type **Maps JavaScript API** and hit enter.
2. Click on the first result, and click the blue **Enable** button.
3. **Required for Street Address Search:** Go back to the search bar, search for **Places API**, click it, and hit **Enable**. (This specific API is what allows the "auto-complete street address search" feature to work).

### Step 4: Verify Your Account (It Stays Free!)
> [!NOTE] 
> **Why do I need to enter a credit card?** 
> Google offers you **$200 of completely free usage every single month**. For a standard store, this covers roughly 28,000 map views a month. It is incredibly unlikely you will ever exceed this, meaning you will pay $0. They simply require a card to verify you are a real person and prevent spam accounts.

1. Google will likely show a warning bar across the top asking you to "Enable Billing" or "Create a Billing Account." 
2. Click it and follow the on-screen steps to enter your details. **You will not be charged.**

### Step 5: Copy Your Key and Hand It Over
1. Once billing is active, look at the left-side menu and click on **Keys & Credentials**.
2. Click on **+ CREATE CREDENTIALS** near the top of the screen, and select **API key**.
3. A popup will appear showing your new API key (it looks like a random string of letters and numbers). 
4. **Click the copy icon** next to it. 
5. Send this key to your developer so they can plug it into your website, and you are 100% finished!
